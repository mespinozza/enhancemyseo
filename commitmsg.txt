Show only blog posts in the blog CMS

/blogs listed 1,553 entries because the `blogs` collection holds both company
blog posts and every article the dashboard and the automations generate, and
the listing query filtered on nothing but userId. The 22 actual posts were
buried, and the page downloaded the whole collection to render them.

Generated articles and blog posts are not otherwise distinguishable: all 1,556
documents carry generation metadata, because the blog has always been written
by generating an article and publishing it by hand. What does separate them is
a slug, which only the blog editor and the site-blog publish path ever set.

So this makes that distinction explicit with an `isSiteBlogPost` flag, set by
both of those paths, and filters the listing on it. scripts/backfill-site-blog-
flag.ts marks the existing posts and has already been run against the project;
it is idempotent and keyed on the presence of a slug.

The sort moved out of the query and into the client. An orderBy alongside an
equality filter needs a composite index, and two dozen documents do not justify
one.
